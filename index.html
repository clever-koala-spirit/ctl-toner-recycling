Frequently Asked Questions

Is the program really free? ▼

Yes, completely free! There are no setup fees, no per-cartridge charges, no collection fees, and no hidden costs. We cover everything because cartridge recycling is better for the planet and we're committed to making it accessible for all organisations.

What cartridges do you accept? ▼

We accept toner cartridges, ink cartridges, toner bottles, fuser units, drum units, print heads, and our signature Enviroliner pens. Essentially, any print consumable from any manufacturer.

How does collection work? ▼

After signing up, we'll send you a free collection box. Fill it with your cartridges at your own pace. When it's full (or on your schedule), book a free pickup through your portal and we'll collect it. Easy as that.

What happens to collected cartridges? ▼

Cartridges are transported to our processing facility where they're sorted, cleaned, and dismantled. Components are separated (plastic, metal, toner) and processed into TonerPlas® material, which is used to manufacture products like roads, pavers, and pens.

How do I get ESG reporting? ▼

ESG data is automatically generated and available in your members portal. You can download reports anytime showing tonnes diverted, CO₂ saved, water conserved, and recovery rates. No extra steps required.

Can I manage everything online? ▼

Yes. Our members portal lets you book pickups, track collections, download reports, and manage your account entirely online. No calls or emails needed (though our team is happy to help if you prefer).

How many cartridges do I need to start? ▼

There's no minimum. Whether you have 1 cartridge or 1,000, we'll help. You can start collecting and book a pickup whenever you have a box full or on a schedule that works for you.

What areas do you service? ▼

We service across Australia. During signup, we'll confirm pickup availability for your location. Most areas are covered, and our team can discuss logistics if you're in a remote region.
